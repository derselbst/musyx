sndSeqAssignVolGroup2Track

void sndSeqAssignVolGroup2Track(SND_SEQID sequid, u8 track, u8 vgroup);

Purpose:

This function assigns a non standard volume group to the specifed track of the given song/sequencer.

Input:

SND_SEQID
ID of song/sequencer to affect.

u8 track
Track to be affected.

u8 vgroup
Volume group to assign to track.

Output:

None.
