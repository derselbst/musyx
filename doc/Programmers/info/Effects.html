Effects System

MusyX has been designed to be very flexible in the way effects are applied to the generated audio signal. Each studio has two auxiliary busses, called AUX A and AUX B, which are each 3 channels wide (left, right, and surround).

Each of the auxiliary busses can have a completely independent type of effect attached to it. You can even put a user-defined effect engine into each of the busses by providing the code for the engine by registering a callback. A couple of the most common effects are provided as part of the system, including reverb, delay, and chorus.

Due to the signal path, all signals passed through the auxiliary busses are delayed by 10ms, even if no effect is active. The PC based emulation offers a plug-in interface. This makes the user-defined effects algorithms available during development time.
